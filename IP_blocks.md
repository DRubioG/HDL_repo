## [Digital AM Radio Reception using Digital LVDS Inputs as 1-bit ADCs](https://github.com/dawsonjon/FPGA-radio)
Modern FPGAs have IO pins capable of differential signalling at rates greater than 1e9 bits/seconds. When configured as an input an LVDS input behaves as a very high speed comparator. In this application, the input is used as an RF ADC. It has a very low resolution of 1-bit, but the high bandwidth allows us to oversample audio signals, allowing us to regain the necessary resolution at audio bandwidths.

- Language: VHDL

## [FPGA-TX](https://github.com/dawsonjon/FPGA-TX)
FPGA-TX is an FPGA based radio transmitter, that can transmit at frequencies up to 400MHz. So far, FPGA-TX supports AM, FM, LSB, USB, Wideband FM, and Wideband FM Stereo.

- Language: Verilog
